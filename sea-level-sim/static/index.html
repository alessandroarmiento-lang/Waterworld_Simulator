Gestures

  • 1 finger east–west spin + north–south tilt (North stays up)
  • 2-finger drag pan
  • 2-finger pinch zoom
  • 2-finger twist clockwise / counter-clockwise (flat roll)
  • Shift + drag same roll (trackpad / mouse)
  • Green emerged · Red submerged
  • Click altitude, city, terrain

Every place shows elevation. Above +135 m the scenario is narrative / not scientific.
